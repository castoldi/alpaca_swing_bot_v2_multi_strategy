# No stop-loss: take profit + re-entry, 2016–2026

**Date:** 2026-10-03 · **Script:** `research/no_stop_experiment.py` ·
**Raw output:** `research/results/no_stop_<strategy>.txt` ·
**Follow-up stress test:** [no-stop-bear-stress-test-2004-2015.md](no-stop-bear-stress-test-2004-2015.md)

## Question

What if the bot never sold at a loss? Every entry keeps its take-profit, the stop
is removed, and a position that never reaches its target just stays open. Re-entry
is the normal rule: once a target fills, the next signal on that ticker can open a
new position. How does that compare with the current strategies (with stops) and
with simply buying and holding?

## Method

- **Variants, fixed before any result was seen** (2 new variants × 6 bracket strategies = 12 trials):
  - **S0 current**: stop + TP + time stop (the time stop only exits at/above entry after N sessions)
  - **N1 no stop + time stop**: TP + the same time stop, so a trade only ever closes at or above entry
  - **N2 no stop, TP only**: hold until the target fills, however long that takes
- **One continuous book**, $5,000 from 2016-01-04 to 2026-09-09, **no January reset**.
  A yearly reset would force-sell underwater positions and act as a once-a-year stop.
- Entries: each strategy's own legacy candidates on Alpaca SIP 4h (one engine for
  every year). Exits re-simulated on the full history with
  `strategies.base.simulate_exit` (S0, N1) or a target-only loop (N2).
- Portfolio: the real engine (`run_annual_portfolio`): 20% whole-share sizing, 5
  slots, one position per ticker, realized compounding. Kill switch off for every
  variant (legacy-engine convention).
- **Yearly returns and drawdowns are mark-to-market on daily closes**, so the paper
  loss on a stuck position counts. Positions open at the end are valued at the last close.
- Buy-and-hold: QQQ, SPY, and an equal-weight basket of the universe names listed
  in January 2016 (NVDA/AMZN/META/AMD), bought once and never rebalanced.

## Findings

1. **In this decade, removing the stop raised returns for all six strategies.**
   The biggest gains were for the weakest strategies (mean_reversion +17% → +311%,
   breakout +162% → +1,031%). Every dip in these five names eventually recovered, so
   a stop usually locked in a loss that waiting would have erased.
2. **The no-stop variants converge.** Ensemble, regime and trend_pullback all end at
   about +1,900–2,250% with about −45% drawdowns and nearly identical yearly rows.
   Once losers are never sold, the entry signal matters much less: the strategy becomes
   "be long these names, bank each bounce".
3. **Risk moves, it doesn't disappear.** Losses turn into stuck capital. Worst drawdown
   rose for 5 of 6 strategies (breakout −12% → −44%, macd −20% → −48%), and 2022 was
   −32% to −42% for every no-stop variant. Today four of the five ensemble slots sit
   at −4% to −40% paper losses.
4. **Buy-and-hold of the same names beat every variant by about 5×** ($605k vs $117k).
   The universe was picked in 2026 and holds the decade's biggest winners. That
   hindsight helps every strategy here, and helps "never sell at a loss" most of all,
   because the variant only works if the name comes back.
5. **Per-trade statistics are meaningless here.** t = 21–32 against a 2.6 hurdle, but
   the 99% win rate only counts closed trades: the losers are still open and never
   enter the statistic. Judge this by the mark-to-market equity curve and drawdown only.

## Verdict

**Not adopted. Recorded for the decision.** The upside is real on this universe in
this decade, but it depends on every held name recovering. The bear-market check is in
[no-stop-bear-stress-test-2004-2015.md](no-stop-bear-stress-test-2004-2015.md).
Before any live use:

- Live mechanics: the current exit is a bracket (TP + SL). TP-only would be a plain
  GTC limit sell. The daily-loss kill switch, the 5-slot cap and the tax guard would
  all interact with positions that stay open for years. None of this was modelled.
- The open question is a name that never comes back (Lucent, Nortel, GE 2008, Intel
  2000). This universe contains none, by construction.
- A middle ground (a wide disaster stop at −25% to −30%, or a maximum hold in months)
  would be new trials and has not been tested.
